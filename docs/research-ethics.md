# Research ethics and data handling

Public accessibility does not automatically grant redistribution rights.

## Do not commit

- source videos or extracted frames without redistribution permission;
- personal data, participant responses, or identifiable contact information;
- confidential drafts, reviewer comments, or unpublished institutional material;
- private filesystem paths, credentials, API keys, or browser exports.

## Recommended practice

- Store provenance and rights information with each case record.
- Use synthetic or openly licensed examples for demonstrations.
- Anonymize case IDs when public disclosure could identify a person or restricted project.
- Distinguish direct observation, interpretation, and contextual evidence.
- Keep a private, access-controlled archive for materials that cannot be published.

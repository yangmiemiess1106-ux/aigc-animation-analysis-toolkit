# Case-selection methodology

Case selection should support the research question rather than reward popularity or apparent visual polish.

## Recommended sequence

1. Define eligibility criteria before reviewing results.
2. Build a candidate pool and record each candidate's source and access status.
3. Exclude cases only with a documented reason, such as insufficient evidence or failure to meet the research scope.
4. Select cases that contribute distinct, comparable visual mechanisms.
5. Keep excluded but informative candidates in a private audit record or an appropriately anonymized public summary.

## Avoid circular selection

Do not select a case because it appears to confirm the eventual findings. Do not use popularity, awards, platform visibility, or perceived quality as a substitute for methodological relevance unless the research question explicitly requires it.

## State the boundaries

The selection record should distinguish eligibility evidence, the comparative role of each selected case, reasons for excluding near-neighbour cases, and limitations caused by unavailable or incomplete source material.

This repository does not claim that its example cases are representative of all generative-AI animation.

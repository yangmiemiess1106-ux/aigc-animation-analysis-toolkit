# Evidence boundaries

The toolkit is intentionally conservative about what visual evidence can establish.

- Promotional copy is not a substitute for direct observation.
- A small number of still frames cannot establish whole-video continuity or dynamic stability.
- A visual result alone cannot prove which AI model, software, prompt, or production process created it.
- Historical or cultural claims may require sources beyond the video and should be cited separately.
- An absent observation means “not observed in the available evidence,” not necessarily “did not occur.”

These boundaries help keep coding records auditable and prevent uncertain inferences from becoming facts.
